# Save-offer policy (synthetic)

## 1. Payment failures

Involuntary (payment) cancellations receive a free payment retry. No discount or credit is offered.

## 2. Service failures

Subscribers with a verified service failure may receive one one-month credit per 12 months.

## 3. New event sign-ups

Event sign-ups in their first 30 days receive onboarding messages only. No discount is offered.

## 4. Exclusions

Kids-only accounts are never targeted (see the kids profile policy). Accounts that have already reactivated are excluded.
