# Kids profile policy (synthetic)

## 1. Targeting

Kids-only accounts are never targeted by retention offers, marketing messages or AI-drafted outreach.

## 2. Enforcement

The rule is enforced in the data layer by a row filter and by the save-segment function, which fails if a kids-only account reaches its output. It is never left to a prompt.
